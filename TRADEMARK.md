## Permitted uses

You may:

- Refer to YMTR by name in a factual, descriptive way.
- State that your project is "based on YMTR" or "a fork of YMTR", provided you
  make clear that it is a modified version and not the official YMTR release.
- Redistribute unmodified YMTR builds with the YMTR name and logo intact, in
  compliance with the applicable license.

## Prohibited uses

You may not:

- Rename a modified YMTR build to "Minecraft Transit Railway" or "MTR" in a way
  that suggests it is the official MTR mod.
- Replace the YMTR name or logo with the MTR name or logo and distribute the
  result as if it were the official MTR mod.
- Remove, obscure, or alter copyright, license, or attribution notices.
- Use the YMTR name or logo in a way that implies endorsement by Yomi_307
  without permission.

## Forks and modified builds

Forks and modified builds must use their own name and icon, or clearly identify
themselves as modified versions of YMTR (for example, "MyPack's fork of YMTR").
They must preserve the copyright and license notices required by the MIT
License and the GPLv3.
